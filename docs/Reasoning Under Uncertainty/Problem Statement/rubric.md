# Project Proposal

A one-page proposal: the problem, why it is genuinely uncertain, the candidate reasoning framework and the alternative it is being chosen over, and how success will be measured.

Criterion | Weight | Emerging | Developing | Proficient
--- | --- | --- | --- | ---
Problem Definition \& Relevance | 25\% | Problem described in general terms; not clear the outcome is actually uncertain. | Concrete and plausibily uncertain, but the source of the uncertainty is not named. | Concrete, and names the specific source of the uncertainty (noise, missing/conflicting evidence).
Framework Selection \& Justification | 30\% | Names a framework with no reason for the choice, or a poor fit for the problem. | Names a framework and a reason, but no named alternative for comparison. | Names a framework, a specific alternative, and a concrete reason for preferring one.
Comparison / Baseline Plan | 20\% | No comparison or baseline proposed. | A comparison is mentioned but no method is named. | A specific baseline or sensitivity analysis is named and briefly described.
Evaluation Plan | 25\% | No metric proposed; success defined only qualitatively. | A metric is named but data/test cases are unspecified. | A specific metric is named along with the data or test cases it will be computed on.
