# Annotated Outline.

A section-by-section outline of the final paper with a short annotation under each heading describing what will go there, plus an initial reference list.

Criterion | Weight | Emerging | Developing | Proficient
--- | --- | --- | --- | ---
Structural Completeness | 30\% | Missing two or more required sections (Overview, Problem Statement, Study Approach, Results, Conclusion, References) | Missing one required section, or sections are out of coherent order. | All required sections present and logically ordered.
Literature Foundation | 30\% | Fewer than 3 sources, or non-technical sources with no annotation | 3-4 credible technical/peer-reviewed sources, each with a one-sentence relevance note. | 5+ credible sources each annotated with how it will be used (background, method, comparison, or data).
Section-Level Clarity | 20\% | Headings only, no description of planned content. | Most sections have a one-sentence description | Every section has a 2-4 sentence description specific enough to predict its content.
Alignment with Proposal | 20\% | Contradicts or omits what was proposed in Deliverable 1. | Broadly consistent, but hasn't incorporated feedback given on the proposal. | Reflects the approved framework, comparison plan and any instructor feedback.
